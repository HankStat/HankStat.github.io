### Bio
---
I am Hank, a Master of Computer Science student at University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, specializing in Distributed Systems and Machine Learning.

I leverage technology to solve real-world problems: 
* AI Application: Constructed GenAI workflows to solve critical customer pain points in object removal, driving **7.5%** of total feature usage. 
* Automation: Engineered automated reminder systems to cut tracking time by **80%**. Established Airflow pipelines for data visualization. 
* Web Development: Developed full-stack dashboards to streamline code monitoring during organizational changes at **15** fabs. 
* DevOps: Implemented automated CI/CD pipelines to reduce code change time by **90%** and ensure system stability. 
* Research: Optimized Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) for fraud detection by **15%** F1-score and fine-tuned LLMs for code generation to **58%** pass@5 rate.

Currently, I am exploring Distributed Systems, with a focus on improving system reliability and resolving flaky tests in Go.
